**Course Description:**
This course studies the implementation of AI in support of organizational decision making. With an overview of historic approaches to Decision Support Systems (DSS) the pathway to today's predictive and autonomous applications for a range of industries is explored.

**Course Outcomes:**
Successful completion of this course will enable the student to:

Explain factors which influence the design, development, adoption, and innovation of AI & ML.
Analyze how the culture, change management, and cognitive psychology impact the historical, present, and possible futures of decision support systems.
Examine the feasibility and impact of AI & ML solutions for organizational decision making from multiple lenses.
Develop written artifacts and presentations for a variety of audiences as part of an AI solution delivery team.
Design multiple prototype solutions for organizational decision making under tight time constraints.
Take part in iterative research, design, development, and enablement of multiple prototype AI solutions in an agile working environment.
Unit Outcomes

Successful completion of the following units will enable the student to:
1.0     Introduction to Decision Support Systems
1.1     Discuss the implementation and techniques of decision support systems.
1.2     Discuss how decision support systems have impacted the equity-deserving groups.
1.3     Explain how culture, change management, and cognitive psychology influence organizational decision making and business transformation.
1.4     Discuss business cases for organizational decision making and business transformation.
2.0     Rapid Prototype Solution Research & Design
2.1     Create annotated research summaries and resource guides.
2.2     Construct prioritized agile project backlogs.
2.3     Use open data and/or synthetic data generation techniques where required.
2.4     Evaluate datasets for AI solutions.
2.5     Design solutions for AI prototypes with and without the use of generative AI tools.
2.6     Demonstrate the role of individual contributor for the agile delivery of multiple AI prototypes related to tactical, operational, and strategic business decisions.
2.7     Take part with peers to prepare and present technical demo of AI prototypes.
3.0     Agile Solution Revisions & Adoption Planning
3.1     Improve on existing solutions based on stakeholder feedback.
3.2     Analyze the barriers to change for AI adoption.
3.3     Create a sample change management plan.
3.4     Build a prototype tool to enable change management.
3.5     Analyze and document the requirements for AI systems and process integration.
3.6     Create a sample integration plan.
3.7     Build a prototype tool to enable integration.
3.8     Identify the requirements for AI solution performance management.
3.9     Create a sample performance management plan.
3.10     Build a prototype tool to enable performance management.
4.0     Ethical Considerations and Prototype Planning
4.1     Analyze AI solutions from multiple lens of the following: accessibility, ethics, equity, privacy, security, sustainability, and well-being.
4.2     Justify the individual contributions towards AI prototype including ideation, design, research, development, and enhancements.
